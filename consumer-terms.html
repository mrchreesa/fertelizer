5. Changing your mind

For consumer purchases made online, by telephone or by email, you may cancel without giving a reason from the time you place your order until 14 days after the day you, or your nominated recipient other than the carrier, receives the goods. If one order is delivered in separate parts, the period runs from the day after the last part is received.

To cancel, tell us clearly by email, telephone or post using the details in section 1. You may use the model cancellation form below, but you do not have to. Sending your cancellation notice before the deadline is sufficient; our acknowledgement is not a condition of cancellation.

Please include your name and enough information to identify the order. Contact us for the return address or collection arrangements. Send the goods back without undue delay and within 14 days after telling us you wish to cancel, unless we have agreed to collect them.

6. Returns and refunds

If you cancel, we will refund the payment for the cancelled goods and the cost of our least expensive standard delivery option. Any extra amount you chose to pay for a faster or more expensive delivery service is not refundable under the change-of-mind cancellation right.

We will refund you without undue delay and no later than 14 days after we receive the returned goods or evidence that you have sent them back, whichever is earlier. If no goods were supplied, or we have offered to collect them, we will refund within 14 days after being told of your cancellation. Refunds use the original payment method unless you expressly agree otherwise, and we will not charge a refund fee.

You pay the direct cost of returning goods when you change your mind only if we informed you of that responsibility before your order. For goods that cannot normally be returned by post, any return-cost estimate you would be responsible for will be provided before you order.

You may inspect the goods as you would in a shop. Where the law allows, we may reduce a refund to reflect loss in value caused by handling beyond what is necessary to establish the goods’ nature, characteristics and functioning. Mixing or applying the product may reduce its value. This does not limit your rights if goods are faulty or not as described.
