Mix, spray, repeat

Envirolizer is sprayed on the leaves, not the soil. It takes a few minutes each time.

  1. 1

    Mix

    Mix 1 teaspoon (approximately 5 g) per 1 litre of water.

  2. 2

    Spray

    Mist both sides of the leaves until they're wet but not dripping. Early morning or late afternoon works best.

  3. 3

    Repeat

    3–5 sprays during the growing period, approximately 15–30 days apart. Repeat for a longer growing period.

Before you spray

A few rules that make every spray count and help you get consistent results.

  • Mix only what you'll use that day.
  • Spray both sides of the leaves until they're wet, not dripping.
  • Spray in the cool of early morning or late afternoon, not in full sun or rain.
  • Keep the pack sealed, dry and out of reach of children and animals.
Mixing and spray schedule

Don't soak the soil

Envirolizer is made to be sprayed on leaves. Pouring it onto the ground wastes the product and won't give the same result.
